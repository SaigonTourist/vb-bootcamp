# Your material

Put what you brought here, then tell Claude: "My material is in input/own, build template C from it."

| What | Example | Used as |
|---|---|---|
| Text of a learning module | `script.docx`, `module.pdf`, `storyboard.pptx`, or pasted text | Key messages, turned into shots and the spoken lines |
| Photos of your places and objects | `training_room.jpg`, `branch_counter.jpg`, `product.png` | References and start frames, so the AI shots look like your world |
| A photo of someone who should appear | `trainer.jpg` | Start frame: the real person speaks in the piece (H3) |
| A short clip you own | `clip.mp4`, 60 s at most | Cut in untouched, between generated shots |
| Your logo or brand colours | `logo.png` | Title and end cards, in the edit |

How to get it here from your browser: on github.com open this repository, go to `input/own/`,
**Add file → Upload files**, drop the files (25 MB each at most), commit. Then ask Claude in your
session to "pull my material". Images can also be pasted straight into the chat.

Never bring client data or anything confidential.
