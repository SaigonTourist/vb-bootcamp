# What to bring on 8 October

In the last part of the day you turn your own material into a short video. Bring one or more of
these, on your laptop or ready to download from your usual storage:

1. **The text of a learning module you work on**: a script, a storyboard, a slide deck or just the
   three key messages. This is the most useful thing to bring; everything else is optional.
2. **Two to five photos of places and objects from your world**: a training room, a branch
   counter, a meeting corner, a product. They make the generated shots look like your bank instead
   of a generic office.
3. **A photo of anyone who should appear**, if you want a real person on screen instead of a
   generated one: a trainer, a colleague. A clear, front-facing photo works best.
4. **A short video clip you own (60 seconds at most)**, if you have one. It goes into the piece
   untouched.
5. **Your logo or brand colours**, for the title and end cards.

Please do not bring client data or anything confidential. Everything you upload is sent to external
AI providers.

Files go into the session through github.com (upload, 25 MB per file at most) or by pasting images
into the chat; we will show you how at the start.
