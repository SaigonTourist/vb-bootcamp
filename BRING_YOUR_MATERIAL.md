# What to bring on 8 October

In the last part of the day you turn your own material into a short video. Bring one or more of
these, on your laptop or ready to download from your usual storage:

1. **The text of a learning module you work on**: a script, a storyboard, a slide deck or just the
   three key messages. This is the most useful thing to bring; everything else is optional.
2. **Two to five photos of places and objects from your world, without people**: a training room,
   a branch counter, a meeting corner, a product, a blank form. They make the generated shots look
   like your bank instead of a generic office.
3. **A short video clip you own (60 seconds at most)**, if you have one, where everyone visible has
   agreed to be filmed for this kind of use. It goes into the piece untouched.
4. **Your logo or brand colours**, for the title and end cards.

Please do not bring photos or videos of colleagues or clients to make them appear or speak, client
data, or anything confidential. Everything you upload is sent to external AI providers.

Files go into the session through github.com (upload, 25 MB per file at most) or by pasting images
into the chat; we will show you how at the start.
