SHOT: wide shot from the lunar surface, as in the start frame, camera locked off, the impact plays in slow motion, 24mm lens, 25 fps.
SUBJECT AND ACTION: the dark red wine bottle drops the last few metres onto the grey dusty ground and slams into it; the glass shatters into hundreds of glittering shards that fly outward in slow motion, a large burst of dark red wine splashes up in a crown and droplets arc away, lunar dust puffs up around the impact.
PLACE: the grey cratered surface of the Moon, small rocks, black sky, the blue Earth small on the horizon.
LIGHT: hard sunlight from camera right, long black shadows, the wine and shards glinting.
COLOUR: grey moon dust, black sky, deep red wine, sparkling glass.
SOUND: a dull heavy boom, the crunch and tinkle of breaking glass, the splash of liquid, then silence.
