# Before/after pairs

Ten pairs for the prompting block, one per strategy. The "before" is how most people write a
prompt the first time: one long sentence, adjectives, famous brands as shorthand, a list of things
not to show, text they want on screen, "the same woman from the previous video". The "after" is
the same intent written with our method. Point at the strategy the pair is named after, then let
the room find the other differences. Every "before" trips the `vg.py lint` warnings of its lesson.

The negation lesson ("say what is there") has no pair: on a short list Seedance 2.5 obeys, and the
effect shows in long rule-heavy prompts. It stays a talking point, with the story of the 70 % rules
prompt that failed the same way on two engines.

Pairs 04, 07, 08 and 10 stay controlled (one thing changes) because they show a mechanism, not a habit.

| # | Pair | Engine | What changes | Say this |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | `01_one_action` | Veo Fast, 6 s, seed 51 | The whole story in one shot vs one action from start to end | A shot holds one physical action; the story comes from the cut. |
| 02 | `02_light` | Veo Fast, 4 s, seed 21 | "Bright, cinematic, 8k, masterpiece" vs one window, camera-left, 4:1 | Adjectives give nothing to execute; a light source with a direction does. |
| 03 | `03_brands` | Veo Fast, 4 s, seed 31 | Ralph Lauren, Starbucks, Apple, Sony as shorthand vs the same look described | Name a brand and its logo gets drawn; the negative prompt does not stop it. |
| 04 | `04_closeup` | Veo Fast, 4 s, seed 41 | Extreme close-up at high energy vs medium shot, small gesture | Big emotion in close-up turns into a grimace; tell it with distance. |
| 05 | `05_text` | Seedance 480p, 5 s | A laptop screen with a budget table, a chart and a contract title vs a closed plain cover | It reads right at a glance: "Kredittract", English mixed in, invented numbers. A bank cannot ship a typo and cannot fix one letter without a re-render. Text goes in the edit. |
| 06 | `06_consistency` | H3, 5 s, 2 takes per side | "The same woman from the previous video" vs one start frame and identical scene text | Described twice is two people. One frame, same words, only the dialogue changes. |
| 07 | `07_filler` | H3, 6 s | Line ends early vs "Also, ja." filler at the end | H3 talks to the end of the clip; give it a harmless last line and cut before it. **Play with sound.** |
| 08 | `08_pose` | Seedance 480p, 5 s + reference | Reference standing vs lying flat, same prompt | The photo decides the pose, whatever the sentence says. |
| 09 | `09_idea` | Seedance 720p, 5 s | "Trust and security" vs the cup turned toward the guest | You cannot film an idea; you can film the small action that makes people feel it. |
| 10 | `10_draft` | Veo Fast vs Veo, 6 s, seed 11 | Draft 0.60 $ vs final 2.40 $, same prompt and seed | Find the shot on the cheap model, render it once on the good one. |

The "after" of pair 09 is the clip rendered through ModelArk on 2 October; everything else is
generated by the script.

## Making them

```bash
python3 scripts/make_pairs.py                plan and cost (about 14 $ for 24 generations)
python3 scripts/make_pairs.py --go           start frame and references first, then every video at once
python3 scripts/make_pairs.py --wait         collect; repeat until nothing is pending
python3 scripts/make_pairs.py --go --redo before --only 01_one_action   after rewriting a before prompt
python3 scripts/make_pairs.py --compose      teaching/pairs/<pair>/pair.mp4, side by side with labels, for the beamer
```

`--compose` makes two files per pair: `pair.mp4`, side by side and muted, to compare the picture, and `sequence.mp4`, before then after full screen with their own sound. Play the sequence for 06 and 07. Before the day,
watch every pair: if a "before" happens to come out fine, regenerate it (a lucky take teaches
nothing), and if an "after" fails, fix it with one change and note what you changed; that is a
story worth telling in the room.
